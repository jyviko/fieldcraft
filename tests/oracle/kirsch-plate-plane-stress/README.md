# Plate with a circular hole in tension, 2D plane stress (Kirsch)

The plate of `kirsch-plate` as a 2D plane stress model: a quarter of half-width 30 with a hole of
radius 1, two quad8 patches around the hole (40 × 16 each, graded 40 : 1 towards it, mid-side
nodes on the arc), symmetry on x = 0 and y = 0, a traction of 1 on x = 30.
