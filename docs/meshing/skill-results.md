# The meshing skill on the corpus and the samples

Generated by `cargo xtask skill-results`; do not edit. Each part is meshed by following
[the meshing skill](../../skills/fieldcraft-meshing/SKILL.md) with today's commands, headless, as
an agent over MCP would (`crates/testkit/src/skill.rs`): `geometry.bodies`, `geometry.features`,
`mesh.plan` and `geometry.check` at the plan's size, `mesh.plan.apply` (which meshes one cell of a
mirror-symmetric body and mirrors it), and where the plan fails the skill's fallbacks in order,
`mesh.hex.generate`, `mesh.hex.project` (sphere template) and then `mesh.tet.generate` (tet10);
the cells of a split part are joined with `mesh.node.merge`; then `mesh.quality.mark` at the
skill's limits (scaled Jacobian ≥ 0.3, edge ratio ≤ 10), `mesh.node.relax` round each marked
element when at most 20 are marked (more is a clustered failure: the blocks are wrong, and relaxing
is not the fix), `mesh.quality.mark` again, `mesh.quality` and `mesh.check.duplicates`. Sizes are
the plan's (1/25 of the body's diagonal, 2 layers through a shell).

Columns: `method` is what meshed the part (`plan (project|cells|sweep)` is the skill's target,
anything else a fallback); `symmetry` the mirror planes `mesh.plan.apply` used; `volume error` the
mesh's volume against the geometry's; `untagged faces` B-rep faces no element face is tagged
with (a set on them finds nothing); `marked` the elements failing the limits before and after
relaxing, and what relaxing changed; ¹ a tet mesh has no edge ratio: its largest aspect ratio
stands in; `duplicates` coincident nodes and cracks; `geometry.check` its findings at the size;
`verdict` pass when the minimum scaled Jacobian and the largest edge ratio meet the limits and
the volume is within 2 % (`pass (fallback)` when a fallback meshed it).
The section pictures cut each part through the middle of its longest side, marked elements red.
Times are wall clock on the machine that ran it.
