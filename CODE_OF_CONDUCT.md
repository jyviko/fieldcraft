# Code of conduct

FieldCraft is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent; criticise work, never people;
- keep discussion technical, specific and on topic;
- not harass, insult, demean or discriminate against anyone, publicly or privately;
- respect the project's decisions on scope, licences and clean-room rules.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block anyone who does not follow
this code. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the GitHub
profile of [@jyviko](https://github.com/jyviko).

This code is our own, written in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
